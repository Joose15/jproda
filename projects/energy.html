Energy Monitoring System

Embedded Systems · Power Electronics · IoT

This project explores the design of a modular energy monitoring system capable of measuring voltage, current and power consumption in real time. The goal was to build a flexible platform suitable for industrial installations and smart energy systems.

The system integrates multiple sensors with a microcontroller capable of performing signal acquisition and digital processing. Data can be transmitted to external systems through communication protocols commonly used in industrial environments.

Hardware Architecture

The hardware design combines current sensing, voltage measurement and signal conditioning circuits. A dedicated ADC captures the signals which are then processed by the embedded firmware.

STM32H7 ADS1115 ADC ACS712 Current Sensor ZMPT101B Voltage Sensor

Signal Processing

The firmware performs signal filtering and RMS calculations to obtain accurate electrical measurements. This allows the system to calculate important metrics such as instantaneous power and total energy consumption.

All measurements are structured in a format suitable for integration with higher level platforms or SCADA systems.

Applications

Potential applications of the system include:

• Smart energy monitoring • Industrial installations • Renewable energy systems • Educational platforms

Conclusion

This project demonstrates how embedded systems can be used to create scalable monitoring platforms for modern electrical infrastructures.
